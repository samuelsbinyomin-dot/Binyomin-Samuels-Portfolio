Book Covers

Charlie and The Chocolate Factory

Front Cover

This book cover was a class project for COM 341, a Communications Design Workshop of the fall semester of 2026. This book cover is for Charlie and the Chocolate Factory. It was a font/text-focused book cover.

For the front cover, I used the font Nickel for the title because it was thick and had a playful energy, and I could use it as a mask for a chocolate drawing I was making. I fused the illustration with the text to give it some character. I positioned the title in the shape of a factory and added smokestacks to reinforce the factory idea. I added light pink clouds to symbolize smoke coming out of the factory, moving across the entire cover on a light blue background to add a childlike feel. I used the font Trogen for the author’s name because it gave off a golden, metallic feel that matched the golden ticket.

Spine

I used Nickel for the title on the spine, and Trajan Color for the author’s name.

Back Cover

For the back cover, I used Georgia for the blurb because it was easy to read and paired well with the font SwashingtonJF used for the tagline. I used the SwashingtonJF font for the tagline because of its fun, childlike look.

Harry Potter and The Philosopher’s Stone

Front Cover

This book cover was a class project for COM 341, a Communications Design Workshop of the fall semester of 2026. This is a book cover for Harry Potter and the Philosopher's Stone. The goal was to make a simple cover using something close to an abstraction.

My idea was to use simplified symbols from the book, like Harry’s lightning scar, glasses, the Hogwarts Express, and a magic wand as simplified illustrations for the book. The front cover features the Thunderbolt scar, train, and glasses. Inside the glasses is the title of the book with the word " Harry Potter in Vatican font and the rest of the title in Bello Script Pro font. I chose both for their handwritten look to evoke a handwritten letter, since that is an important part of the story. I used the Corundum Textbook font for the author's name on the front cover.

Spine

I used the Vatican and Bello Script Pro fonts the same way for the spine title. I also used the Corundum Textbook font for the author's name on the spine.

Back Cover

I used the Corundum Textbook font for the back-cover blurb. I also created a magic wand with sparkles that ran from the tip of the wand on the back cover, moved to the spine, and continued to its final destination on the front cover.
